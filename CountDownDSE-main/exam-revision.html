The complete revision process

Use AI to build a complete exam revision process

Diagnose mistakes → Guided learning → Targeted quiz → Personalised revision notes

This process helps you first find what you really don't understand, then fix your weak areas step by step, and finally use a quiz and notes to confirm you have truly mastered them.

01

Upload your mistakes or materials first

Let AI see exactly where you went wrong

First upload one of these materials:

  • Homework or worksheets with mistakes
  • The related textbook pages

AI does not help you revise by guessing. It analyses what you most need to fix based on the mistakes you actually made.

It is best to work through one question at a time, so AI can more accurately find the weak point behind each one.

Click to copy Prompt 1: Generate a learning diagnosis report
02

Generate a learning diagnosis report

Find your strengths, weak points and what to fix

AI will help you analyse three things:

  • What you have already mastered
  • Why you went wrong
  • Which part you should fix first

The aim of this step is not to criticise yourself, but to find the weak points most worth revising.

Key point: don't just ask "What is the answer?". Ask "Why did I get this wrong?" first.

03

Fix weak areas with Guided Learning

Don't look at the answer. Learn it step by step.

First switch Gemini's mode to:

Guided Learning

Then use Prompt 2. Based on the weak points just found, AI will ask you questions step by step like a tutor, guiding you to work things out yourself.

The most important things in this step:

  • When you answer wrongly, look at the hint first. Don't rush to the answer.
  • Take your time, and only move to the next concept once you understand this one.
Click to copy Prompt 2: Start Guided Learning
04

Take a targeted quiz with Canvas

After learning, check whether you have really mastered it

Switch Gemini's mode to:

Canvas

Then use Prompt 3 to generate a 5-question quiz focused on your weak points.

Suggested standard:

  • Below 80%: go back to Guided Learning and work on it again

Really learning something does not mean reading the explanation. It means getting it right again by yourself.

Click to copy Prompt 3: Generate a targeted quiz
05

Generate personalised revision notes

Record what you got wrong, learned and tested this time

Finally, use Prompt 4 to ask AI to generate revision notes that are entirely your own.

The notes should focus on:

  • Key revision points
  • A record of your mistakes

These notes are not ordinary textbook content. They focus on your own mistakes and weak points.

Click to copy Prompt 4: Generate your own revision notes
06

The complete process

Each round fixes one more weak point

Upload mistakes or materials → Generate a diagnosis report → Guided learning → Canvas quiz → Personalised revision notes

Don't give AI an entire subject all at once. Focus on one topic or one type of mistake each time. You will see more clearly where you went wrong, and find it easier to really understand.

Each round through the process leaves you with a set of your own revision notes. Once you have built up a few, you can combine them before the exam into one set of revision notes that suits you best.

完整溫習流程

用 AI 建立一個完整的考試溫習流程

診斷錯題 → 引導學習 → 針對小測 → 生成個人化溫習筆記

這個流程幫你先找出真正不明白的地方，再逐步補弱項，最後用小測和筆記確認自己是否真的掌握。

01

先上傳錯題或教材

讓 AI 先看清楚你真正錯在哪裏

請先上傳以下其中一種材料：

  • 做錯的功課或工作紙
  • 課本相關頁面

AI 不是靠猜測幫你溫習，而是根據你實際做錯的地方，分析你最需要補救的部分。

建議最好一題一題處理，讓 AI 更準確找出每一題背後的弱點。

按此複製提示詞一：生成學習診斷報告
02

生成學習診斷報告

先找出強項、弱點和補救方向

AI 會幫你分析三件事：

  • 你已經掌握了甚麼
  • 你為甚麼會錯
  • 接下來應該先補哪一部分

這一步的目標不是批評自己，而是找出最值得溫習的弱點。

重點：不要只問「答案是甚麼」，而是先問「我為甚麼會錯」。

03

用輔助學習補弱項

不直接看答案，而是一步步學懂

請先把 Gemini 模式轉為：

輔助學習 / Guided Learning

然後使用提示詞二。AI 會根據剛才的弱點，像補習老師一樣逐步提問，引導你自己想通。

這一步最重要的是：

  • 答錯時先看提示，不要急着看答案
  • 不要太心急，學懂後才進入下一個概念
按此複製提示詞二：開始輔助學習
04

用 Canvas 做針對性小測

學完之後，要驗證自己是否真的掌握

請把 Gemini 模式轉為：

Canvas

然後使用提示詞三，生成一個針對弱點的 5 題小測。

建議標準：

  • 80% 以下：回到輔助學習，再補一次

真正學會，不是看完解釋，而是能夠自己再做對。

按此複製提示詞三：生成針對性小測
05

生成個人化溫習筆記

把今次錯過、學過、測過的內容記錄下來

最後使用提示詞四，要求 AI 生成一份只屬於自己的溫習筆記。

筆記應該集中整理：

  • 溫習重點
  • 錯題記錄

這份筆記不是普通課本內容，而是針對你自己的錯題和弱點。

按此複製提示詞四：生成專屬溫習筆記
06

完整流程

每完成一次，就是補好一個弱點

上傳錯題或教材 → 生成診斷報告 → 引導學習 → Canvas 小測 → 個人化溫習筆記

不要一次過把整科內容丟給 AI。 每次集中處理一個課題或一類錯題，你會更清楚自己錯在哪裏，也更容易真正學懂。

完成一次流程，就留下一份屬於自己的溫習筆記。 累積幾份筆記後，考試前就可以合併成一份最貼合自己的總溫習筆記。
