01

Start with the right mindset

Learn better with AI

AI does more than help you finish questions. It helps you master how to solve them yourself.

It is natural to turn to AI when you are stuck on homework. But AI can do more than give you the answer. It can help you understand a question step by step, until you can finish it yourself.

Learning well with AI does not mean jumping straight to the answer. It means using hints, explanations and guidance to build your own understanding.

You have only really learned it when you can explain the method yourself and complete a similar question again.

02

How to ask

Don't just ask: "What is the answer?"

Avoid asking like this:

  • Do this question for me.
  • Just give me the answer.
  • What is the answer to this question?
  • Finish all my homework for me.

This may be quick, but you may not know what the question is testing, or what to do next time.

03

Better ways to ask

Ask instead: "How should I think about this?"

Try asking like this:

  • I have no idea where to start. What concept is this question testing?
  • I have got this far. How should I think about the next step?
  • Is there anything wrong with my method?
  • Can you give me a hint first, without giving the answer?
  • Can you explain it in a simpler way?
  • Can you give me a similar question to try again?

These questions help you keep your own thinking process, rather than just copying down the AI's answer.

04

Instant answers vs real learning

Finishing a question is not the same as learning it.

Real learning means you know:

  • what concept the question is testing;
  • what information the question gives you;
  • how to begin the first step;
  • where you went wrong along the way;
  • whether you can do it again yourself when a similar question comes up.

The greatest value of AI is not helping you "skip the difficulty", but helping you "face the difficulty".

05

Remember when using AI

AI can help you, but should not replace you.

  • AI can explain things to you, but should not think for you.
  • AI can give you hints, but should not complete your homework for you.
  • AI can help you check your work, but it is not always correct.

In the end, you have only really learned it if you can do it again yourself without looking at the answer.

01

先建立正確心態

用 AI 學得更好

AI 不只協助完成題目，更幫助你掌握自行解題的方法。

遇到不懂處理的功課時，使用 AI 協助是很自然的事。但 AI 不只可以給你答案， 它更可以幫助你一步步理解題目，最後能夠自己完成。

真正有效的 AI 學習，不是直接跳到答案，而是透過提示、解釋和引導， 幫你建立自己的理解。

當你能夠自己說明解題方法，並且可以再完成一次類似題目， 才代表你真的學會。

02

提問方式

不要只問：「答案是甚麼？」

不建議這樣問：

  • 幫我做這題。
  • 直接給我答案。
  • 這題答案是甚麼？
  • 幫我完成整份功課。

這樣做可能很快，但你未必知道題目考甚麼，也未必知道下一次應該怎樣做。

03

更好的問法

改為問：「我應該如何思考？」

建議這樣問：

  • 我完全不知道如何開始，這題考甚麼概念？
  • 我做到這一步，下一步應該怎樣想？
  • 我這個方法有沒有錯？
  • 可不可以先給我一個提示，不要直接給答案？
  • 可不可以用簡單一點的方法解釋？
  • 可不可以出一條類似題讓我再試一次？

這些問法會幫助自己保留思考過程，而不是只抄下 AI 的答案。

04

直接出答案 vs 真正學習

完成一題，不等於學會一題。

真正學習，是你知道：

  • 這題考甚麼概念；
  • 題目給了甚麼資料；
  • 第一步應該怎樣開始；
  • 中途錯在哪裏；
  • 下次遇到類似題目時，自己能不能再做一次。

AI 最有價值的地方，不是幫助你「跳過困難」，而是幫助你「面對困難」。

05

使用 AI 時要記住

AI 可以幫你，但不應該代替你。

  • AI 可以幫你解釋，但不應該代替你思考。
  • AI 可以給你提示，但不應該直接完成你的功課。
  • AI 可以幫你檢查，但它不一定永遠正確。

最後，如果你能夠不用看答案，自己再做一次， 才代表你真的學會。
